Event Scoring Reference

Cornhole: Cumulative points. First to 11 wins each game. Best of 3. Winning game score is 11; losing team keeps its points.

Kubb: Cumulative points. Each kubb = 1 point. King = 5 points. Max 10 per game. Best of 3.

.22 Shoot: Each player gets 5 shots. Add both players’ scores together.

Hatchet Throw: Each player gets 3 warm-ups, then 6 scored throws. Add both players’ scores together.

Bucket Golf: Scramble format. Under par adds points. Over par subtracts points. Example: -2 becomes +2; +3 becomes -3.

Mystery Event: Trampoline toss. Add both teammates’ successful throw points together.
