Rules Snapshot

Cornhole: Two round-robin games. Score = game points + 5 per win. Max 32.

Kubb: Two games. 2 points per kubb + 5 for king. Max 30.

Hatchet: 5 scoring throws. 2 points per stick, 5 for bullseye stick.

Trampoline: Successful throws from 5, 10, 20, and 40 point lines.

Bucket Golf: 2 points per stroke under par; -2 per stroke over par.

Shooting: 5 shots. Score = hits times selected points per hit.
